# Solo healer introduction

Scope: `/wow-healer/` and its image-led homepage invitation. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: people who remember playing a healer in WoW / World of Warcraft and want to understand this independent mobile game's actual healing play.

Action: inspect the real gameplay and follow the official localized App Store or Google Play badge.

Established world: inherit the incumbent sanctuary, hand-painted stone/forest imagery, teal surfaces, warm gold emphasis, Hahmlet headings, Noto body text, existing header/footer, and official badges. This extends the site and does not replace its identity.

Composition: an original healing-party panorama with live copy and an actual localized combat screenshot in the first viewport; a raid screenshot with three concrete healing decisions; alternating equipment and guild passages; concise factual FAQ and a final store action. The homepage receives an illustrated invitation rather than a keyword list.

First viewport: make both the fantasy healing connection and the actual mobile interface visible. The page names its own independent game and does not present the illustration as gameplay.

Signature interaction: every actual gameplay image opens a keyboard-accessible, scrollable full-size viewer; closing returns focus to its trigger. The content is readable without animation or opening the viewer.

Build: code-led for this delegated implementation, anchored in the existing site. No approved image comp and no standing build-path default are recorded. The user explicitly delegated implementation choices through the cooperating Healer project; no duplicate preference interview is required for this brief.

Review: desktop/mobile in all three languages, screenshot viewer, FAQ, navigation and store links; HTML metadata, self-canonical/hreflang, shared image, JSON-LD and sitemap parity. Search Console submission and ranking measurement are outside the available evidence.
